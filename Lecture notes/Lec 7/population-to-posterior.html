Three worked examples, one pipeline

The same reasoning chain, run three times: a fabrication line, a clinic, and a monitoring fleet. Each stage below builds on the last — and at every choice point, there's a question you can try before revealing the reasoning. The first two end in a discrete random variable (PMF/CDF); the third ends in a continuous one (PDF/CDF) instead.

population & sample→ descriptive stats & the right chart→ events & counting→ Bayes on one test→ two tests & a random variable→ PMF/CDF or PDF/CDF
Built for ENDG 319 · conditional probability, counting, Bayes' rule, and random variables — discrete and continuous — in one thread.
