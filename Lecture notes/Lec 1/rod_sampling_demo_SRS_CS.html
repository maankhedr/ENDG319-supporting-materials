Switch between the two sampling methods above, then draw — SRS pulls uniformly from all 1000 rods; the convenience method only ever pulls from the early-production zone (rods 1–120), the way an engineer grabbing "the first 50 off the line" would. Run each method out to 200 or 2000 draws and compare the running-average lines: the SRS line settles onto the true p, while the convenience line settles just as tightly onto the wrong number. More data fixes sampling variation — it does nothing for sampling bias.
